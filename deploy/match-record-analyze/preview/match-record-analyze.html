摆好机位再开始，球速、落点与线审更稳。Frame the court first for steadier tracking.

录制中请保持 / While recording
手机固定、勿遮挡；尽量飞行模式。Keep phone locked; prefer airplane mode; both baselines in view.

球速为估测值，误差约 ±10%。Speed estimate ±10%.
