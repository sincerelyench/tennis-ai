ZQ-149 · WeChat login auto-fills 微信号 (wechatId)

What is written into 微信号
---------------------------
On POST /api/auth/wechat success, if profile.wechatId is empty:
  wechatId = sanitizeWechatId(openid)
  (typical WeChat openid already matches ^[A-Za-z0-9_-]{3,32}$)
  If openid fails sanitize (charset/length), wechatId = "w" + sha256("wxid:"+openid)[:31]

nickName is NEVER used as wechatId (displayName only).

Why openid
----------
WeChat mini-program official APIs do not return the user's real 微信号 string.
Product preference: after WeChat login, 完善资料 must not force re-entry of 微信号;
「我的」微信号栏 should show a value. openid-derived handle satisfies gates;
users can replace it with a real contact WeChat ID in 「我的」 anytime.

Also
----
- wechatContactSatisfied: explicit wechatId OR bound openid
- getSession lazy-backfills wechatId for already-bound accounts
- MP shell stores wechatId from login response (miniprogram/utils/session.js)
